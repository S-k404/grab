Settings

Download folder

With sub-folders, playlists also get their own folder inside the site folder.

Parallel downloads

4–8 is a good range. If a site starts failing with “429 Too Many Requests”, lower it.
One download at a time per site, with short random pauses, so your account isn't flagged for automated activity. Other sites still download in parallel.

Speed limit & schedule

e.g. limit to 2 MB/s during the day so calls and streaming stay smooth, full speed at night.
Links added outside the window wait for it; “Start now” overrides it. The Mac must be on and Grab open.

Logins

“Brave extension (shared)” is the easiest: the Grab extension shares your logins for YouTube, Instagram, Reddit, X, TikTok, Facebook and Threads only, with no system permission needed. Other browsers can be read directly, but macOS may block that (then Grab tells you).

Stream Catcher extension

Spots the video streams a page plays (HLS, DASH, MP4) and sends them to Grab in one click. It also shares your logins. For Brave, Chrome, Helium and other Chromium browsers.

  1. Open brave://extensions (Chrome: chrome://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
  2. Click Load unpacked and choose the extension folder: Documents → site-download → browser-extension.
  3. Pin Grab Stream Catcher to the toolbar.

Send to Grab button

Drag this to your bookmarks bar. On any page, click it to download that page straight away.

Send to Grab

Can’t drag? Click Copy code, add a new bookmark and paste it into its URL field (not the address bar).

Updates

Updates install only when nothing is downloading, then the engine restarts in a second or two. Downloads that failed because yt-dlp was out of date are retried automatically.

Tools

Log

Grab writes what each download did, and why any failed, to a small log file (3 MB at most). On a failed download, Copy details copies everything needed to look into it, never your logins.

Auto-fix

When a download fails, Grab checks why and retries with a fitting strategy. What worked is tried first next time on that site.
